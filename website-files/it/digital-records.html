Digital Records & Documents – Customer and Auditor Screen

Confirmation that documents and records can be kept on a controlled digital platform: ISO 9001:2015 · IATF 16949:2016 · VDA 6.3 (process audit)

Standards alignment – the position

Confirmed: none of the three standards requires shopfloor documents or records to be handwritten or kept on paper. Electronic media is acceptable when the documented-information controls, retention rules, traceability and any customer-specific requirement (CSR) are met.

Interpretive briefing, not a certification-body determination. Before any paper record is retired, review the customer's CSRs and contract.
